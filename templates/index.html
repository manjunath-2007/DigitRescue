How It Works

Step 1

Normalize Input

Uploaded PNG or JPEG images are decoded with OpenCV, converted to grayscale, inverted if dark-on-light, and formatted to 28x28 white-on-black pixels.

Step 2

Dual Restoration Branches

A selected classical OpenCV filter runs as a baseline while the blind denoising autoencoder reconstructs clean strokes directly from the damaged input.

Step 3

CNN Recognition

The same trained CNN classifies the original, classically preprocessed, and AI-restored digits, reporting top-3 softmax probabilities for each.
